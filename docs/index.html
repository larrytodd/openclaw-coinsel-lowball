Coinsel Lowball Check

An OpenClaw plugin that tells you whether a cash offer for a coin is fair, what to counter with, and where to get more — before you say yes.

Try the Lowball Check on Coinsel View source on GitHub

What it does

Tell your OpenClaw agent what a dealer, pawn shop, or coin-show buyer offered you. The plugin checks recent sold comps, the PCGS price guide and live melt value through Coinsel, a price-intelligence service for graded coins and currency, and returns:

  • A verdict: lowball, below fair, fair, or strong
  • What the coin is actually worth, and where that number comes from
  • Melt value for silver and gold coins
  • A typical fair dealer-offer range, adjusted for raw vs. certified coins
  • A counter-offer and exactly what to say
  • Nearby coin dealers and upcoming coin shows when the offer is low
  • How buyers in your area typically price, once enough local data exists

Install

openclaw plugins install openclaw-coinsel-lowball

No API key or configuration needed.

Example

You: The pawn shop offered me $28 for an 1881-S Morgan dollar, PCGS MS63. I'm in 60025.

Agent: 🚩 Lowball: $28 for 1881-S Morgan dollar (MS63). Retail value $110 (PCGS price guide, MS63). Melt value $44.22. The offer is 25% of retail; a typical fair dealer offer is $66–$88.

Counter at $85. What to say: "PCGS guide is $110 on this coin. I can do $85 today."

Where to get a better offer: a dealer 2.3 miles away, and a coin show this weekend.

Later, tell your agent what happened ("I got $80 at another dealer") and it logs the result.

Tools

ToolWhat it does
coin_lowball_checkPrices the offer. Required: coin, offer_usd. Optional: grade, certified, grading_service, zip, buyer_type, offer_channel, sale_reason.
coin_offer_outcomeLogs what you did afterwards: sold, countered, sold elsewhere, or kept it, and the final price.

Where the numbers come from

Certified coins are priced against recent sold results and the PCGS guide; you can look up any slab yourself with the PCGS cert lookup. Raw gold and silver are priced against melt, which is a floor rather than a market price. If you're shopping rather than selling, Coinsel also lists certified coins priced below guide value.

Privacy and data use

Each check is stored anonymously: the coin, grade, certification, offer amount, buyer type, how the offer was made, the reason for selling if you mention it, the first 3 digits of your ZIP code, and any outcome you report. Names, contact details, full addresses, and which dealer made an offer are never collected.

This data powers the regional comparisons, and Coinsel may publish or license aggregated statistics built from it. Aggregates are never based on fewer than 5 offers, and individual checks are never sold. Full policy: Coinsel privacy policy.

Limits

Free, rate-limited per IP. Values are estimates from public sales and price-guide data, not appraisals or grades.
